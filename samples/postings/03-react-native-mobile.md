# Mobile Engineer (React Native) — Fieldnote (fictional)

Fieldnote is a note-taking app for field researchers, used offline in remote places. Remote, Europe time zones.

## You will

- Build new features in our React Native app for iOS and Android.
- Improve offline sync and app start-up time.
- Release to the App Store and Google Play every two weeks.

## You have

- 4+ years of experience with React Native.
- TypeScript.
- Experience with offline-first data (SQLite, WatermelonDB or similar).
- Shipped and maintained apps in both app stores.
- Experience profiling and fixing performance issues on mobile.

## Nice to have

- Native iOS (Swift) or Android (Kotlin) experience.
- Expo.
- A bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field.

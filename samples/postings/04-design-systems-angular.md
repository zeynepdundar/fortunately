# Design Systems Engineer — Halvard Software (fictional)

Halvard builds document-management software for mid-size companies. Munich, hybrid.

## The role

Our design system team maintains the component library used by 12 product teams. You will build accessible components, run our design-token pipeline and partner with designers.

## Must have

- 4+ years of frontend development.
- Angular (v17 or later) and TypeScript.
- Deep knowledge of accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA).
- Experience maintaining a shared component library with versioned releases.
- Experience with Storybook.

## Should have

- Design tokens and tooling such as Style Dictionary.
- Visual regression testing.
- Figma, including working with the Figma API.
- Web Components or Lit.

## Good to know

We cannot offer relocation support or visa sponsorship for this role; you need to be based in Germany already.

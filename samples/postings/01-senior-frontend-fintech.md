# Senior Frontend Engineer — Lumora Pay (fictional)

Lumora Pay builds payment tools for small online shops across Europe. We are hiring a Senior Frontend Engineer to join our Merchant Dashboard team (remote within the EU).

## What you will do
- Own features in our merchant dashboard end to end, from design review to release.
- Evolve our component library together with our designers.
- Improve performance and reliability of pages that handle money.

## What you bring
- 5+ years of professional frontend experience.
- Strong React and TypeScript skills.
- Experience with a modern data-fetching library such as TanStack Query or SWR.
- Solid testing habits: unit tests and end-to-end tests (Playwright or Cypress).
- Experience building or maintaining a design system.
- Fluent English.

## Nice to have
- Next.js App Router.
- Experience with payments or other regulated domains.
- GraphQL.
- Some Node.js on the backend.

## What we offer
Remote-first team, learning budget, 30 days of holiday.
